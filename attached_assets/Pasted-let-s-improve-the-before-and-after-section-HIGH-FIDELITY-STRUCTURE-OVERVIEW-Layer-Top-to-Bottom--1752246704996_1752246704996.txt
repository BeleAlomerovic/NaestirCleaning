Your card should feel like a product showcase tile, not a content block.

🔲 Size & Position
Width: 720px to 900px
Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 4:3 (no awkward cropping)
Centered horizontally, but padded from top/bottom to breathe
🎨 Visual Treatments
Element Recommendation
Border radius 16px or 20px — consistent with your brand UI
Shadow 0 20px 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.08) — soft, atmospheric
Overflow hidden — ensures all video/image animation stays contained
Background Transparent OR lavender-tinted (#f6f0ff) card layer

VIDEO BACKGROUND: TEXTURE + LIFE
This video is not a feature — it's a texture of credibility. It makes the experience feel alive.

Technical Setup:
HTML <video> element
Props: autoplay, loop, muted, playsinline
Set object-fit: cover to ensure it fills container
Use filter: brightness(0.75) or overlay gradient for text readability
Overlay Gradient (recommended):
background: linear-gradient(to bottom, rgba(255,255,255,0.2), rgba(0,0,0,0.3));
l Advanced Additions:
A slow zoom (scale: 1.05 → 1.1) animation on the video

You want the user to feel like they're uncovering cleanliness.

Option A: Wipe Reveal (Most Cinematic)
On hover or drag:
A horizontal or diagonal wipe slowly removes the before image
A thin dividing line tracks the wipe, creating a "cleaning stroke" feel
The after image and video peek through
🎯 This mirrors the gesture of wiping a dirty window — direct sensory analogy.
How to Implement:

Use a div mask over the before image
